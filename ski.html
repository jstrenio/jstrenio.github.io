Ski Career
For nearly 15 years I competed internationally and filmed video segments for ski movies. Contest highlights included winning a bronze medal in the X-Games and the World Ski Invitational, 5 time Rails to Riches Winner and placing 6th in the first ever FIS World Championships, the event that led to the inclusion of slopestyle skiing into the Winter Olympics.
